# Country and territory gardens — next five proposals

The owner clarified that U.S. amusement-park work should remain separate and requested continuing the other 252 country/territory garden identities. This first review batch adds Thailand, Myanmar, Malaysia, Indonesia and the Philippines. The same builders are connected to actual arena scenes and the existing world-tour view; these are not five disconnected mockups.

All five retain two benches, story-seat anchors, the existing harmless botanical paint-flower/sprinkler painting corner, and a freestanding Garden Park **Coming Soon** sign in front of the future entrance. No rides, lazy rivers, neighborhoods or U.S. state parks were changed.

## Designs

| Place | Garden identity | Visible differences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thailand | Lotus pools and golden-shower courtyard | Two lotus reflecting pools flank a level causeway; hanging yellow flower chains, orchid stands and timber shade seating. |
| Myanmar | Upland rose walk and orchid collection | Circular promenade, layered rose blossoms, branching upland conifers and orchid planters. |
| Malaysia | Hibiscus and torch-ginger promenade | Five-petaled hibiscus with projecting staminal columns, tall torch-ginger bracts and a slatted shade shelter. |
| Indonesia | Orchid shade-house and fern collection | Offset garden loop, dark stone edging, brick approach, open orchid shade-house and arching fern fronds. |
| Philippines | Jade-vine gallery and sampaguita beds | Long overhead pergola, hanging turquoise curved-claw flowers and white sampaguita beds. |

These are original, stylized botanical-garden interpretations, not replicas of real parks or assertions that every ornamental plant is native to its assigned country. The same fantasy paint plants are retained everywhere by owner direction; their fictional paint behavior is separate from real botany. The visual materials retain the established outdoor treatment. This does not make every garden asset photorealistic.

## References checked

- Thailand's golden-shower flower form: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassia_fistula — hanging racemes and five yellow petals; its Thai cultural association informed the garden, not a copied park layout.
- Myanmar: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Kandawgyi_Botanical_Gardens — Pyin Oo Lwin's botanical collection, roses and orchids. This is a secondary reference; attempted official tourism pages were inaccessible during this pass.
- Malaysia flower form/cultural association: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibiscus_rosa-sinensis. Forest collection and paved-route context: https://www.frim.gov.my/attractions/nature-activities/kepong-botanic-gardens/.
- Indonesia's botanical-garden setting: official operator https://kebunraya.id/r/bogor and https://kebunraya.id/r/bogor/interesting-spot. The page confirms the garden/operator context; the modeled orchid/fern arrangement is our own proposal, not an exact documented collection inventory.
- Philippines: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strongylodon_macrobotrys — turquoise, hanging claw-shaped jade-vine blooms. Kew's linked jade-vine page and the National Museum search were inaccessible; those pages are not represented as successfully verified sources.
