## Project and judging checklist

From the supplied screenshots, the four judging categories each carry 25%: execution, creativity, usefulness/value, and polish/thoughtfulness. The top twenty advance for another review using those criteria. Three winners are listed for $1,000 each.

Prioritize a reliable, understandable demo over claiming every planned park is complete. The official mission page describes a publicly hosted, reusable multiplayer game joined by room code from a phone or laptop, without required account login or app installation. Preserve guest play alongside PAINTFALL's optional saved accounts. Demonstrate real multiplayer; bots alone do not establish it. Verify the challenge-tool/build workflow against the mission and describe actual tool use honestly.

## Conduct and content review

The supplied Challenge Code of Conduct requires original work made with the challenge tool, respect for intellectual-property rights, accurate project claims, and appropriate conduct toward participants. It prohibits harmful/illegal/discriminatory/explicit material, harassment, hate speech, harmful misinformation, fraud, plagiarism, account misuse, and violent imagery or graphic descriptions of violence.

PAINTFALL review items before entry:

- Review existing combat/zombie presentation, weapon labels and effects against the conduct rules. Do not assume that all fictional combat is automatically permitted, or silently redesign approved mechanics during documentation work.
